Executive summary & vision

Anyone joining Ofia — product manager, engineer, designer, marketer, investor, or operations lead — should be able to read this document and understand what Ofia is, how it works, how it makes money, and what gets built next.

Not this

"A marketplace"

A place that just lists businesses and takes a commission undersells what Ofia actually gives a business — and misframes every downstream product decision.

This

A multi-tenant business operating ecosystem

Businesses receive digital tools, a storefront, operational software, customer relationships, and access to a wider discovery network — all under one tenant record.

Foundation

Problem statement & proposition

Nigerian SMEs don't lack ambition — they lack an affordable, unified way to run a business digitally. Ofia's proposition is deliberately blunt: one business, one ecosystem, multiple tools.

What businesses currently juggle

  • Paying developers separately for a website
  • A separate POS system, inventory system, and accounting tool
  • Separate HR software and separate logistics arrangements
  • Separate marketing tools with no shared customer data
  • Difficulty building or affording a mobile app
  • Difficulty acquiring and retaining customer relationships
  • High staffing costs to manage fragmented subscriptions
  • No access to major commercial locations or business credibility

Ofia's proposition

One business. One ecosystem. Multiple tools.

A single tenant record gives a business a storefront, operational software, a Compass discovery profile, payments, logistics, and AI — configured through feature flags, not custom development.

    Part IV · 03 Engineering

    Multi-tenancy & identity

    One person can simultaneously be a customer, employee, merchant, and administrator — identity stays singular, roles stay contextual.

    Tenant record

    Tenant ------ id business_name vertical_id subscription_plan enabled_modules theme_id status

    Contextual roles, one identity

    User A Customer → Fashion Shop A Employee → Company B Merchant → Company C // Identity remains one user. // Roles are contextual.

    Centralized identity service handles authentication once; every other service resolves tenant and role context from that single user record rather than maintaining parallel logins.

    Engineering

    Event architecture

    Major actions emit events; services subscribe where relevant instead of calling each other directly — this is what keeps twelve product families from becoming one tangled monolith.

    ProductCreated ProductUpdated OrderCreated BookingCreated PaymentCompleted CustomerFollowed InventoryUpdated

    A new module can subscribe to existing events (e.g. AI Service listening for OrderCreated to trigger a reorder suggestion) without the Order service knowing AI exists.

    Part V · Engineering

    Frontend architecture

    Templates never fork per tenant — configuration, theming, and feature flags compose a tenant's experience on top of one shared template.

    Template + Tenant Configuration + Theme + Feature Flags + Data = Tenant Experience

    Design system

    Shared buttons, inputs, cards, modals, navigation, typography, icons, and design tokens across every product surface.

    Theme engine

    primary_color secondary_color font border_radius button_style layout_variant

    Feature flag system

    Tenant │ ├── Payments ✓ ├── Preorders ✓ ├── Loyalty ✗ ├── AI Marketing ✓ ├── SMS ✓ └── Advanced Analytics ✗

    The backend must enforce these permissions, not just hide buttons in the UI — a disabled module should reject the API call, not merely be invisible.

    Reference

    Glossary

    Ofia's own vocabulary — naming choices that carry the ecosystem philosophy, not just labels.

    Tenant
    A single business's root record — the unit every module scopes access to.
    not: account
    Vertical
    A registered, context-specific marketplace experience (cars, food, hotels).
    not: category
    Compass
    The business discovery and marketplace layer, independent of a Shop.
    not: directory
    Shop
    The commerce-focused, customer-facing storefront on .ofia.shop.
    not: store page
    Office
    A business's operational and digital presence on .ofia.ng.
    not: dashboard
    Follow
    A customer's subscribed relationship to a business, replacing the need for that business's own app.
    not: subscribe / like
    Module
    An independently deployable ERP or platform capability, gated by a feature flag.
    not: plugin
    Business Hub
    A physical pickup, storage, and vendor space extending the platform offline.
    not: warehouse
