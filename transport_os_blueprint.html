Product Strategy & Architecture

Transport Operating System

Four interconnected codebases forming one complete transportation ecosystem.

Four CodebasesTransport OSShared InfrastructureAdmin/ERP/Mobile

Product vision: I’d structure this as one transportation operating system with shared core screens plus service-specific screens, rather than designing independent products.

Core principle: The key is that the screens should make the architecture obvious: Super Admin provisions → Transport Company configures → customers book → drivers execute → fleet/operations manages → finance measures.

Implementation Plan & Repository Mapping

The system is consolidated into four major codebases mapped directly into the existing ecosystem:

ofia_admin

1. Super Admin (/fleet)

Controls transport companies, provisioning, subscriptions, and platform-wide data.

ofia_erp

2. Company Admin & Ops

The company's command centre (erp/admin/mobility) and real-time operations console (erp/ops/mobility).

mobile_mobility

3. Customer App & Portals

Single app handling passenger booking and specialized portal access (school/corporate tracking).

mobile_driver

4. Universal Driver App

One unified driver application for every transport service type.

Baked into ofia_erp. erp/admin/mobility is the backend command center. erp/ops/mobility is for live dispatchers.

Top KPI cards

  • Revenue today, Trips today, Active trips, Bookings, Available vehicles, Vehicles on trip, Drivers available, Drivers on trip

Fleet status

Live Operations Map

  • Clicking a vehicle opens: Bus, Driver, Service, Trip, Destination, ETA, Passenger count

  • Vehicle Profile: Identity, Status, Operational Performance (Utilization, Fuel), ROI

  • Profiles: Personal, Employment, License, Current assignment

Pricing Engine, Finance & Reports

Pricing Engine & Simulator

Distance × Fuel Cost + Driver Cost + Maintenance + Tolls + Days + Other Costs + Markup = Customer Price

Simulator allows admins to calculate expected profit and margin for hypothetical trips.

Fleet Intelligence & Profitability

  • Health Dashboard: Vehicles scored based on Age, Mileage, Downtime, ROI (Excellent, Healthy, Watch, Replace).
  • Profitability: Calculates if a bus is generating revenue but destroying margin due to maintenance/fuel.

Finance Dashboard

  • Transactions, Receivables, Payables, Driver Payouts, Refunds, Commissions.
  • Customer Payments (Paid, Pending, Failed, Refunded).

Reports & Settings

  • Operational, Financial, Fleet, Customer, Service comparison reports.
  • Global Settings: Roles, Enabled Services, Payment Providers, Notification Templates.

App 3: Customer App & Portals (mobile_mobility)

Built into a new mobile_mobility directory sharing the mobile_customer UI. Handles individual passengers, corporate clients, and parents.

Customer App & UX Flows

The app is fully white-labelled. Interfaces dynamically adapt based on the specific services enabled by the Transport Company.

Onboarding & Authentication

  • Dynamic Splash: Instantly loads the tenant's brand colors, logo, and a tailored onboarding carousel highlighting only their enabled modules.
  • Guest Preview: Users can browse routes and pricing without an account, prompting login only at the point of booking.
  • Smart Corporate Auth: Work emails automatically map users to their Corporate Portal/Staff Transport plan without needing a manual toggle.
  • Progressive Profiling & Biometrics: Collects basic details (Phone/SSO) first, prompting for DOB/Gender later via gamified Home screen prompts. Supports FaceID/TouchID for quick re-entry.

Universal Home & Live Ride

  • Dynamic Service Grid: A modular layout (e.g., minimal 3-column icons or large dual cards) that scales perfectly depending on how many services the tenant has active.
  • Context-Aware Active Trip: If a ride is ongoing or a shuttle is arriving soon, an interactive widget (ETA, vehicle plate, mini-map) dominates the top of the Home screen so users don't have to dig into their "Bookings" tab.
  • Intelligent "Where to?": Time-and-location-aware quick prompts (e.g., a one-tap "Going to Work?" button at 7:30 AM).
  • Wallet & Rewards: Prominent display of user balances, points, and gamified loyalty progress.
  • Live Screen: Driver photo/rating, Vehicle plate, Live map, Call/Message, Trip status updates.

Service-Specific Flows

Rental

Select vehicle → Enter route → Dates → Pay → Booking QR

Shuttle

Select route/date/stop → Seat map → Select seat → Pay → QR Ticket

Interstate

Origin/Dest → Date → Departures → Select seat → Pay → QR Ticket

On-Demand

Pickup/Dest → View ETA/Fare → Request Ride

Corporate & School Portals

Corporate Client Portal

For staff transportation and recurring corporate accounts.

  • Dashboard: Active routes, Employees, Today's attendance
  • Employees: Assign routes, Suspend transport
  • Billing: Monthly invoice, Usage, Contract tracking

School / Parent Portal

A parent should not see the transport operator's entire system.

  • Child Dashboard: Today's transport, ETA, Pickup status
  • Notifications: "Bus is 5 mins away", "Child has boarded", "Child arrived at school"

Notifications & Customer Profile

  • Channels: Push, SMS, Email, WhatsApp.
  • Events: Booking confirmation, Driver assigned, Trip started/completed, Delay, School/Corporate alerts.
  • Profile: Saved locations, Payment methods, Tickets, Invoices, Support chat/tickets.

App 4: Universal Driver App (mobile_driver)

Built into a new mobile_driver directory sharing the mobile_rider UI. Extremely simple interface adapting to any service.

Driver Interface & Workflows

Driver Home & Assignment

  • Large status button (GO ONLINE / OFFLINE).
  • The assignment screen adapts based on the incoming job:
    • Rental: Customer name, Time, Destination.
    • Shuttle / Interstate: Route, Stops, Passenger Manifest.
    • School / Staff: Employee/Student list, Pickup points.
    • On-Demand: Passenger, Pickup, Fare.

Universal Trip Flow

Assigned → Accepted → On the way → Arrived → Boarding → Trip started → Trip in progress → Trip completed

Boarding & Operations

  • QR Scan: Passenger presents QR; Driver scans to verify and mark "Boarded".
  • School/Staff: Driver taps "Boarded" or "Dropped Off" next to names on the manifest.
  • Incident Report: Quick actions for Accident, Breakdown, Medical, Police. Operations receives it immediately.
  • Earnings: Today's/Weekly earnings, Commission, Pending payout.

The screens may look like many modules, but underneath, they're mostly different views of the same operational objects.

The Big Design Principle

I would not build Rental, Shuttle, Staff, Interstate, School, or Uber as independent systems.

I'd build a Transport Operating System with configurable service types.

Rental = Booking + Vehicle + Driver + Pricing + Trip
Shuttle = Route + Schedule + Seat + Booking + Vehicle + Driver + Trip
Staff = Contract + Employees + Route + Schedule + Vehicle + Driver + Trip
On-Demand = Request + Dispatch + Vehicle + Driver + Trip + Pricing

"Bring your transport business. We provide the technology."

A company can launch with one service, such as shuttle, and later activate rental, interstate, school transport or on-demand without replacing its entire technology stack.
